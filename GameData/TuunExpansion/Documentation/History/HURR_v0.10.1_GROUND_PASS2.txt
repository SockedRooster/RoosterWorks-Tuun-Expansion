Hurr v0.10.1 - Ground Pass 2

Based directly on the surface screenshots from the v0.10.0 test.

Goal:
Break up the smooth, uniformly painted appearance at landing distance without
changing Hurr's approved global geography or orbital appearance.

Changes:
- PQS maxLevel 10 -> 11
- more aggressive close-range subdivision
- much tighter floor/cliff texture tiling
- steeper terrain begins blending to cliff material sooner
- stronger contrast and slightly darker ground material
- primary procedural relief moved to higher spatial frequency
- added a second low-amplitude, very high-frequency PQS noise layer for
  lander/rover-scale bumps and surface breakup

Atmosphere/science from v0.10.0 are retained unchanged.

Test target:
Land in roughly the same area as the previous screenshots and compare the
surface at 5 km, 1 km, 100 m, and landed camera height.
