Hurr v0.12.2 - Regional Color Ground Pass

Purpose:
Preserve Hurr's large-scale colored regions when descending into the atmosphere
and when landed, while retaining seamless Parallax close-ground detail.

Changes:
- Rebuilt Parallax influence map as low-influence RGBA.
- Hurr's VertexColorMap/global color now dominates the local terrain color.
- Repeating Low/Mid/High/Steep textures converted to mostly neutral rocky detail carriers.
- Reduced remaining specular/environment reflection.
- Seamless v0.12.1 texture architecture retained.
- Scatterer atmosphere, EVE clouds/haze, Kopernicus height/PQS geometry unchanged.

Expected result:
A dark orbital region should remain dark/brown on approach and on the ground;
a lighter/rust region should remain visibly lighter/rust instead of both resolving
to the same peach terrain material.
