TuunExpansion v0.20.16 - Dot Surface Pass TEST

First dedicated Dot surface-scatter pass. Adds Parallax/Dot_Scatters.cfg
with DotCarbonRocks, a sparse dark rock population based on the existing
Bub rock config and its stock Duna slab meshes/textures.

Settings: seed 2116, spawnChance 0.035, populationMultiplier 2,
scale 0.10-0.65, range 300 m, LOD transitions at 75 and 150 m.
Terrain color sampling remains enabled; neutral gray tint 0.48 and
environment factor 0.10 reduce the bright/tan appearance of the source
material. Actual color and density depend on lighting and Parallax settings.
Rocks align to terrain normals and avoid steep slopes using the existing
scatter slope filters. Collision level remains 1 from the source config;
actual collision behavior depends on the installed Parallax settings.

Reviewed Dot's existing body, height/color maps, terrain material paths
and PQS integration. Terrain geometry, maps, orbit, biomes, science and
Parallax displacement (zero) are preserved for this first controlled pass.

Baseline: uploaded TuunExpansion(3).zip, SHA256:
7772401A1229F48D52386EA124DE4F28EC35016471646079D47A54400A8CEA08
This restores the uploaded pre-v0.20.14 wind-isolation state, removing
the v0.20.15 oak trunk experiment. No independently labeled v0.20.13
archive was available; the upload's exact version is unconfirmed.
All 367 baseline files remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

Lukas tree flicker remains unresolved and deferred in TUUN_ROADMAP.md.

Install: replace the existing GameData/TuunExpansion folder with the
TuunExpansion folder from this ZIP, retaining installed dependencies.
Requires PromisedWorlds, ParallaxContinued and the stock scatter assets
already referenced by the expansion. Restart KSP fully before testing.

Test Dot in daylight at several sites (basins, midlands and highlands).
Check that rocks appear sparsely and sit on the surface, without hovering
or sinking. Walk/drive around them and approach/recede across 75, 150
and 300 m while panning the camera. Check collision behavior and frame
rate. Report excessive darkness, density, popping/flicker or startup errors.

Package checks validate structure, local asset paths and archive contents.
External stock scatter assets and actual rendering require the user's
KSP installation. This is an in-game test build, not a verified visual fix.
