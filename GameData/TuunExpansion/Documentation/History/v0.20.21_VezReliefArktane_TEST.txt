TuunExpansion v0.20.21 - Vez Relief + Arktane Surface TEST

Based on v0.20.20. Dot and all unrelated bodies remain unchanged.

Vez:
- Approved ground material/texture settings are unchanged.
- Height edits are confined to the Highlands biome; all other height
  pixels, including accepted Basins, are byte-for-byte unchanged.
- New shorter-scale spherical relief spans roughly 6-26 km features,
  with up to +/-1260 m detail strength, upper-height headroom and a
  feathered 16-pixel interior biome boundary. No maximum-height clipping.
  Relative to v0.20.20 highlands change by -2009 to +212 m; this creates
  deeper valleys and stronger elevation variation, not uniform uplift.
- New Cache/Vez_v02021.bin path requests scaled-terrain regeneration.
- Rock populationMultiplier 4 -> 8 (intended 2x population); spawnChance
  remains 0.40. Render limits 720/2000/4800 -> 1440/4000/9600.
- Rock albedo/normal now reference Vez's approved regolith maps with
  neutral tint and no extra terrain-color multiplication. Source color
  matches the ground; different lighting/UVs may affect perceived color.
- Rock sizes, seed, range, LOD distances and slope filters unchanged.

Arktane first surface pass:
- New 1024x1024 square cool gray icy regolith and matching wrapped normal
  detail/neutral shader data replace tiled miniature global maps.
- All four tiers use this texture; bump scale 1.20 -> 0.65; displacement
  stays zero. Existing other ground material settings remain unchanged.
- Rocks use the same regolith albedo/normal, neutral tint and no extra
  terrain-color multiplication. spawnChance 0.055 -> 0.40; population
  multiplier 2 -> 4; maximum scale 0.45 -> 0.85. Render limits raised
  to 720/2000/4800. Seed, range, LODs and slope filters unchanged.
- Terrain height/color/biome maps, geometry, atmosphere, orbit and science
  are unchanged. Albedo generated with the built-in image generator;
  exact prompt included in Arktane_Regolith_PROMPT.txt.

Install: replace GameData/TuunExpansion with the folder in this ZIP and
restart KSP. Retain dependencies and allow Vez cache regeneration.
Reposition your highland test craft onto the changed ground. Check Vez
highlands across several sites, accepted basins, rock color/coverage and
performance. Then inspect Arktane plains, cryobasins, uplands and poles
for seams, material brightness, rock placement/density and performance.

Heightfield, material paths and ZIP checks passed. Intended population
multipliers are not guarantees of rendered counts. Both passes require
in-game testing. Lukas tree flicker remains deferred.
